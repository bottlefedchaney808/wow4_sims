# Paladin

Last verified: 2026-09-19 · status: **alpha (official recap)**[4]

Only class with a full Blizzard write-up. Use this as the Forever class-design template.

## Identity kept

No interrupt. Weaker general slows. Taunt lives on **Judgment while Seal of Fury is up**, not a dedicated taunt button. Strong vs Undead/Demon (Exorcism, Holy Wrath, Turn Undead). Fewer ranged tools vs other types.[4]

## Class-wide

| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| Holy Strike | Level **6**, instant weapon strike, Holy damage, **12s CD** |
| Seals | **Not consumed** by Judgment |
| Seal of Fury | New tank Seal: wants fast weapons, small absorbs, Judgment **taunts** while active |
| Consecration | **Baseline level 20**. High dmg+threat on first **4** targets, lower after |
| Blessing of Kings | Baseline (was a talent gate) |

## Holy

- Improved Holy Strike — shorter Holy Strike CD
- Voice of Truth — temp Silence/Interrupt immunity
- Reverence — Spirit mana regen **while casting**
- Infusion of Light — Holy Shock / FoL crits reduce Holy Light cast time
- Holy Shock — **10s CD**
- Consecrated Ground — enemies in Cons take more Holy
- Light's Vigil — expensive CD: reset Holy Shock; extra damage **or** party heal through an ally

## Protection

- Improved Seal of Fury — mana when Fury absorb hits
- Shield Specialization — mana on block
- Swift Judgment — reset Judgment **once/min** (missed taunt recovery)
- Templar's Bulwark — big HP-based absorb, Forbearance interaction
- Reckoning — also from **blocks** (works while crit-immune)
- Iron Creed — timed Holy Strikes reduce incoming damage briefly

## Retribution

- Vindication — reduces enemy AP **and** increases the paladin's
- Sacred Arbiter — empowers Holy Strike, refreshes Judgments
- Champion of the Light — spell damage from Intellect
- Instrument of the Law — less threat, Holy Wrath **instant**
- Twist of Light — Seal twisting without a swing-timer addon: new Seal echoes into next melee swing
- Optional Holy Shock ranged/spell path

## Sim implications

- Unified hit/crit helps Ret/hybrid.[4]
- Fast-weapon Prot (Seal of Fury) vs slow-weapon classic Prot — model weapon speed as first-class.
- Seal twist is now an in-game echo, not an addon mechanic.
- Consecration is a 4-target cleave with falloff — AoE cap in the engine.
- Healing gear carries 1/3 SP; Holy questing is less dead.
