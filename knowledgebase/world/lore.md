# Lore frame

Last verified: 2026-09-19 · `official`[6][7]

- Separate continuity from Modern WoW.
- Clock: after WC3 *Forsaken Kingdom*, before Molten Core / Kazzak reopening the Dark Portal.[6][7]
- Forsaken Paladins: mistrusted, learning the Light in undeath. Bandarion Keep, Whispering Wood, Tirisfal. Level-60 epic mount quest in the name of **Retribution** (Alliance paladin charger = Redemption).[6]
- Skyborne / shen'dorei: Highborne rebels fled Eldre'Thalas after War of the Ancients, pact with wind spirits, island anchored in Skywall. Spirits gone, pylons failing. Windshapers vs High Order vs Al'Aketh.[6]
- Hyjal: post-Archimonde recovery, immortality already spent.[5]
