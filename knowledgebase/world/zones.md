# Zones

Last verified: 2026-09-19 · `official`[5][6] + `wowhead`[17][9]

Original EK + Kalimdor remain. Four **new** regions at launch. Existing zones get extra quests (starting areas, Wetlands, Desolace centaur).[6]

## New regions

| Zone | Level | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zephras Isle** | 1–12 | Skyborne start | Floating island, Skywall-inspired. Crisis: pylons failing, wind spirits gone. Factions: Windshapers (Horde), High Order (Alliance), Al'Aketh (antagonist). Pack-gated.[1][5][6] |
| **Riverglades** | mid-30s–mid-40s | leveling frontier | ~size of STV. 150+ quests (Found Photos also says nearly 200).[5][6] Borders Burning Steppes, Redridge, Swamp of Sorrows, Badlands. Powderfuse Port + goblins, gnolls, ogres, Twilight's Hammer, Brotherhood of the Horse. Hosts Krol'dok.[6][17] |
| **Shen'dralas** | (level band `unknown`) | Kalimdor mid/late | Between Mulgore and Desolace via Valley of Bones. Shen'dralar / Eldre'Thalas / Dire Maul / centaur. Quests send you to Maraudon and Razorfen Downs.[5][17] |
| **Mount Hyjal** | 60 | endgame outdoor | Aftermath of Archimonde. Darkwhisper Gorge. Hosts Hyjal Summit; one Barrow Deeps entrance.[5][17] |

## Travel (`official`)[6]

New ships:

1. Stormwind Harbor → Auberdine (early EK↔Kalimdor)
2. Menethil / Southshore / Auberdine extra flexibility
3. Tanaris Steamwheedle → Powderfuse Port (Riverglades)

No flying.[5]

## Visual pass

Updated lighting on classic zones; Classic preset can revert feel.[1][17]
