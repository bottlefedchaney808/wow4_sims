Last sweep: 2026-09-19. Confidence tags on every claim. Empty sections are intentional stubs.

## What Forever is

A **third, permanent WoW** that sits beside Modern and Classic. Vanilla 1–60 Azeroth, **level cap stays 60**, no flying, no level scaling. New zones, quests, dungeons, raids, a new race, reworked classes/racials/itemization, and ongoing patches. Included with a WoW subscription.[1][5][8]

Not a Classic patch. Not SoD. Fresh characters, no announced transfers.[27]
