# Racials (original eight)

Last verified: 2026-09-19

Design: **two active + two passive** per race, similar offensive budget, distinct utility.[4]

## Officially walked through[4]

### Dwarf

| Racial | Forever |
|---|---|
| Stoneform | Still clears/immunes Bleed, Poison, Disease. Defensive is **−Physical damage** not +Armor |
| Find Treasure | Can stay **on with other tracking** |
| Mace Specialization | **+crit with all spells and abilities** while a mace is equipped |
| Big Game Hunter | **+damage vs Beasts** (Wowhead: 5%)[11] |

### Undead

| Racial | Forever |
|---|---|
| Will of the Forsaken | Removes Charm/Fear/Sleep; **no longer grants immunity** |
| Cannibalize | Restores **Mana and Health** |
| Underwater Breathing | unchanged |
| Touch of the Grave | new: attacks/spells can drain life |

## Wowhead beta tooltips[11]

Use as `wowhead` until Blizzard articles drop. Several "Benefits" column notes on that page are stale (author said they're fixing). Effects column is the useful part.

**Tauren:** Cultivation (bonus herbs, no herb skill needed), Endurance (+5% health **and +1% hit**), Plainsrunning (speed stacks while moving), War Stomp 2s.

**Orc:** Axe Spec (axes increase spell **and** ability crit), Blood Fury **+10% AP and SP for 15s**, Shatter Curse (curse/bane immune + less magic dmg 8s), Hardiness −20% stun duration.

**Troll:** Beast Slaying +5% vs Beasts, Berserking +10% haste 10s, Regeneration 10% of health regen in combat, Rapid Regeneration (50% max health over time).

**Gnome:** Eureka! (next 3 spells cheaper and +10% dmg/heal), Expansive Mind (max resource), Engineering Spec (more reliable gadgets), Escape Artist (brief root/snare immune).

**Human:** Will to Survive (stun break, full tooltip TBD), Perception 20s stealth detect, Sword Spec **+2% spell and ability crit** with swords, Human Spirit +5% Spirit.

**Night Elf:** Elune's Light +10% crit 15s, Wisp Spirit +75% speed while dead, Quickness +1% dodge **and +2% run speed**, Shadowmeld stealth while immobile.

Sim: racials are no longer "Orc melee / Troll caster" only — weapon specs give **spell crit** too.[4][11]
