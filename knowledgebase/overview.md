# Overview

Last verified: 2026-09-19

**World of Warcraft: Forever** is Blizzard's permanent Classic-era game, announced at BlizzCon 2026 on September 12, 2026.[8] It is a **new client / ruleset**, not a Classic patch and not Season of Discovery.

## Same vs new

**Kept (Classic feel):** 1–60 curve, nine classes, threat/CC dungeon play, ~10–15s solo mob TTK, no dungeon finder announced, factions stay separate.[4][5][27]

**New:** realmless rulesets, Camping, Legacy, opt-in transmog, HD/SD models, gamepad, merged hit/crit, new stats, 10/20-man raids, Darkspear Islands BG, professions +600 recipes, Skyborne, six new race/class pairs, four new regions.[4][5]
