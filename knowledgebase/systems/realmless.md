# Realmless rulesets

Last verified: 2026-09-19 · `official`[4]

No classic realm picker. Pick a **ruleset** (large ecosystem, not one megaserver):

| Ruleset | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | launch | PvP needs consent |
| PvP | launch | world PvP in contested space; faction balance systems "similar to SoD" |
| Roleplaying | launch | RP overlay |
| Hardcore | after launch | enclosed; death can transfer the character **out** to another ruleset including PvP |

Cross-ruleset grouping: **no**. Horde/Alliance grouping: **no**.

Two-part character names, unique **per region** (example "Ana Forever"). Language preference matching.

Sim note: no mechanical impact except Hardcore (consumable/death constraints later). PvP vs PvE buffs unknown.
